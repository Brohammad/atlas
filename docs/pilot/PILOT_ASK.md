# Pilot ask — 20 engineers (P-01)

This is the script for the first regression cohort. It does not require accounts. Do not send a rubric. Do not sit with the participant while they work; a result produced while the author watches is not a valid finish gate (G2).

## URL to send

**Track (recommended ask):** https://atlas.brohammad.tech/classical/regression

Participants may also enter from https://atlas.brohammad.tech/ and follow the line → gradient-descent → regression path. Direct links to later units remain reachable; the ask is still to finish **unit 1 (a line with a bias)** and reply.

## Instructions to send (copy)

> Open the regression unit at the link above. Work through it on your own (phone or desktop). When you finish, Atlas gives you a short result sentence on the last screen — copy it and send it back to us.
>
> If you missed any station, read the idea text Atlas shows after each miss before you send the sentence.

## Reply we need

Paste the **exact** result sentence from the unit (for example the text from “For the reply” on the result screen). It includes whether you cleared the unit and which stations you missed, if any.

## One follow-up question (G3)

After you receive their paste, ask exactly one question:

> **When you missed a question, did the explanation after the miss describe the mistake you actually made?**

Record yes / no / partly in your notes. This is the G3 gate in `docs/product/PRODUCT_METRICS.md`. It is not a survey product.

## What we are not asking in this pilot

- Time on page, streaks, or “did you enjoy it”
- Whether they would pay (G6 is unsolicited comments only)
- Transfer items (G4) — sent separately after the sitting, not in this message

## Local debugging only

On a machine running the app from the repo, `/api/events` and `/review` can help debug a sitting. Production does not keep the event file. Do not quote production event counts.
