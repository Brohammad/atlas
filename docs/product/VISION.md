# Vision

Atlas teaches a working engineer to do the next correct thing with a model they could otherwise only call. A sitting is finished when the learner can predict, compute, diagnose, and transfer. A certificate, a streak, or a video watched to the end does not count.

The line that already governs the product:

**Learn the operation. Predict the result. Break the procedure. Fix the specific mistake. Face a case you have not seen.**

## What Atlas is

A lesson-first lab. Each unit is a short sequence of questions whose wrong answers are wrong procedures, with the arithmetic locked so the miss is conceptual. Feedback names the learner's mistake. The math lives in tested TypeScript (`lib/`), and the page is a client over that math.

Live product: [https://atlas.brohammad.tech](https://atlas.brohammad.tech).

## What we refuse to become

A catalog. The Traditional ML outline of about 140 lessons across 12 modules is an inventory of topics, not a commitment to build them. Publishing lesson counts for unbuilt modules already misled the map once. Those counts stay out of the interface.

A video course, a documentation site, or a pile of generated tutorials. Those products measure time-on-page. Atlas measures whether the next answer was right for a reason the learner can repeat on a new instance.

A second product that restates the same pitch inside the lab. The public page sells the sitting. The sitting starts at the first question.

## Tracks

Traditional ML is the only track in progress. AI Engineering, Deep Learning, Generative AI, Backend, Distributed Systems, and Systems Programming are names. Opening one of them before a stranger can finish regression and come back is a new product, not an extension.

## Commercial end state

A paid track is plausible only after we know three facts:

1. Engineers who are not the author finish a unit.
2. They get a later, unseen case right.
3. Someone, learner or employer, will pay for that evidence.

Until those are observed, Atlas is a free cohort. Subscriptions, team licenses, and proctored assessments are hypotheses recorded in the PRD. They are not roadmap items.

## Standard

The product is good when an engineer who only knew `model.fit()` can explain a residual, a partial, a step, and a failure on a new point, and can do it a week later without the hints. Looking impressive is irrelevant.
